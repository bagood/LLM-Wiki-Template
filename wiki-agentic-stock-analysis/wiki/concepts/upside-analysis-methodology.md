## Definition

The upside specifications combine supplied technical features with dated news/catalysts to assess exactly five or ten future exchange sessions. They require twelve sections, three final bullets and a disclaimer, separating generation, analysis, news and technical times. Their vertical-field output restriction describes the source report workflow, not wiki formatting.[^stock-upside-analysis-5-instructions-7ddbeb4e49d6506f][^stock-upside-analysis-10-instructions-510cb15a884ac7ad]

## Evidence and scenarios

Technical JSON is the sole source for numerical market/indicator inputs. News and official-calendar/status research are allowed in the described upstream workflow, but online market-data replacement is excluded. Single bars with derived history and provisional calendars can still support the best-qualified forecast, with limitations disclosed. Bull/base/bear outcomes describe the final-session close, and subjective probabilities are not claimed calibrated.[^stock-upside-analysis-5-instructions-7ddbeb4e49d6506f][^stock-upside-analysis-10-instructions-510cb15a884ac7ad]

The September 25 ICBP 5dd report separates improving rebound hints from a strong bearish trend and distribution. HRTA retains positive short momentum and supply news alongside weak trend strength, incomplete participation and prospective dilution. EPAC 10dd preserves buyback sentiment against losses, capital-action tension and extreme liquidity uncertainty.[^icbp-c79c85e00a548f9d][^hrta-30a4845bd9a6638a][^epac-8a9e4ccf50586c57]

BULL’s 5dd and 10dd reports share the September 24 close but provide different terminal bands, risk references and illustrated economics. The 10dd methodology adds first-week versus second-week sequencing and a session-5 checkpoint without resetting expiry.[^bull-b65b7de46fe80d87][^bull-270816fba3cce331][^stock-upside-analysis-10-instructions-510cb15a884ac7ad]

## Limits of the collection

Detailed reports describe one supplied OHLCV bar, unavailable foreign/non-regular data and uncertain adjustment/indicator definitions. Their embedded remote citations and compliance assertions are preserved report claims, not independent verification performed during wiki creation.[^icbp-c79c85e00a548f9d][^hmsp-6718bdc124e03030][^epac-8a9e4ccf50586c57]
