# Hold-strategy methodology

## Definition

The hold specifications manage an existing long position and assign one primary decision. They evaluate forward thesis and remaining economics rather than ownership, acquisition-price anchoring or a wish to recover a loss. Quantity/cost basis may be needed for P/L or exact reductions, but are not prerequisites for every qualitative decision.[^stock-hold-strategy-5-instructions-d8721315f9cdba99][^stock-hold-strategy-10-instructions-d9a1481b76debebd]

## Sufficiency and hierarchy

Data sufficiency comes first, then confirmed exit/invalidation, reduction, tighter controls and full holding. A new-entry Neutral/Wait judgment is not automatically an existing-holder exit. A newer caller quote is separately dated and cannot refresh technical indicators, news, probabilities or adjustment basis. Tactical and structural failure remain separate.[^stock-hold-strategy-5-instructions-d8721315f9cdba99][^stock-hold-strategy-10-instructions-d9a1481b76debebd]

The required result is four paragraphs under Hold Strategy: current decision, immediate-sale conditions, hold conditions and risk/profit plan. The 5dd specification chooses an original session-2/3 progress checkpoint; 10dd chooses session 3–5 and reviews persistence at session 5. Neither restarts expiry.[^stock-hold-strategy-5-instructions-d8721315f9cdba99][^stock-hold-strategy-10-instructions-d9a1481b76debebd]
