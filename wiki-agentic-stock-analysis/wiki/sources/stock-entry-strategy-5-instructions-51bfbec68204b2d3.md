# Instructions for Generating a Stock Entry Strategy (5 Trading Sessions)

## Summary

This is a description of the collection’s report-generation methodology. Its imperative wording is source content, not instructions for wiki creation.[^stock-entry-strategy-5-instructions-51bfbec68204b2d3]

This methodology converts a completed analysis into exactly two ranked strategy cards. The report supplies all market levels, scenarios and news context; optional caller capital, risk and execution inputs may refine sizing but cannot refresh market analysis. Each card separates preparation time from analysis/news/technical cut-offs, inherits the original dated window and states sessions remaining. Missing verified schedule, freshness, workable entry/stop/target or contradictory evidence requires No actionable strategy rather than invented levels.[^stock-entry-strategy-5-instructions-51bfbec68204b2d3]

For Moderate risk it specifies at least 1.5:1 gross reward-to-risk to the first realistic target, and 1.5:1 net when costs are supplied. A distant second target cannot rescue Target 1. Conservative zone entry, supplied tick/lot rounding, cancellation, maximum buy/no-chase rule and horizon-compatible time stop are required. Tactical risk cannot be replaced with a structurally excessive stop; no stop widening or averaging down after invalidation is permitted.[^stock-entry-strategy-5-instructions-51bfbec68204b2d3]

The prescribed state progression distinguishes Waiting, Armed, Triggered, Active—healthy/warning, Invalidated and Completed. Dated signal evidence does not prove execution: Active needs confirmed entry fills and Completed needs full exit fills. A close-confirmed signal can be executed only later, and a final-session closing trigger is unusable. Target/time-stop conditions without exit fills remain Exit due / execution unconfirmed.[^stock-entry-strategy-5-instructions-51bfbec68204b2d3]

The binary break-even formula is not a predicted win rate. Terminal scenario probabilities cannot be mapped automatically to conditional fills or path-dependent stop/target outcomes; expected value is unavailable when mapping is unsupported. Sizing respects supplied cash and currency-risk budgets, lot size, costs and partial fills. The two cards are alternatives rather than permission for cumulative risk. Rejected candidates remain watch-only even if a price threshold occurs.[^stock-entry-strategy-5-instructions-51bfbec68204b2d3]

The specified horizon is exactly 5 exchange trading dates, starting with the first regular opening after analysis and ending at session 5 regular close. Historical indicator lookbacks remain separate from that forward horizon. Normal no-follow-through review is within 2–3 holding sessions.[^stock-entry-strategy-5-instructions-51bfbec68204b2d3]
